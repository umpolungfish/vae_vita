vita-probe --melt: the freeze ladder

At fixed seed the word is piecewise-constant in temperature (frozen
draws, moving thresholds), so the melting curve is exactly computable:
bisect the breakpoints, print every plateau's word and gate, and at each
break the token that flipped with its frozen prefix. Seed 666 resolves
to 35 plateaus over 0.05..1.6 at 0.005 resolution in ~20s: the cold
archetype is the bare closure ⊢>◇×●⊣, the prefix ⊢>◇ never melts across
the whole range, and the verdict sequence along the ladder walks through
all four phases including a re-entrant clean-T band in the middle heats.
