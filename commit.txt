vita_native plank 1: the Grammar-native substrate begins.

vita_native/src/alphabet.rs — the closed IMASM symbol alphabet, 100 tokens:
the 16 opcodes (classic 12 plus the SIXTEEN_3 extension ☊ ☋ ~ ≁, glyphs and
order per imasm16_3.rs), the 16 SIXTEEN_3 register states as single tokens
(canonical Reg16_3 names; B included as the FOUR/Belnap render of TF), the
12 primitive letters, the 48 Shavian letters, and the structure marks with ?
as the malformed-input witness. One entry, one token, one meaning; encode is
longest-match, decode is its exact inverse (the tokenizer is itself a
lossless split-fuse), ROTAT and canonical_rotation are library operations.
Seven tests, all green.

vita_native/src/gen.rs — vita-gen, the procedural data generator. Samples
program-shaped words (VINIT-opened, TANCH-sealed, delta/mu balanced), labels
every one through the imasm oracles: eval (FOUR flow + sink), eval16
(SIXTEEN_3 flow + sink), prove (kernel verdict as Belnap token: green=T,
else B held). Emits glyph-native JSONL with flow signatures (per-dyad ⊙=id,
×=lossy), sink readouts, canonical rotation, and token ids. Never
hand-imscribed: the tools are the only labelers.

Probe batch confirms the classical-slice correspondence in the data itself:
sink4 B pairs with sink16 A, F with Ff.

Next planks: the word-template (turn = ⊢ ... δ query arm ... ⊞ held thinking
... μ answer arm ... ⊣), then the candle trunk (ROTAT positions, split-fuse
attention with mu.delta=id orthogonality, 12 layers, S11 latent core).

Plank 2: template.rs — the word-template. A dialogue turn IS an IMASM word:
⊢⟨query⟩◇⊞⟨thinking⟩●⟨answer⟩⊣, with optional ⊙⟨context⟩ imscription before
the opening. The skeleton was put to the oracles BEFORE any content rode it:
kernel green against BelnapSplitFuse, dyad lossless (id), sink B. ⊞/ENGAGR
is the thinking switch with kernel semantics (the held dual between fork and
fuse; the empty block is the hard off-switch). render/parse are exact
inverses (mu.delta = id on the template itself), segments nest so primitive
tuples ride inside arms untouched, three render modes (complete, generate-
with-thinking, generate-direct), and the ⊣⊢ adjacency between turns stands
on the kernel-proved bifurcation law. Six template tests + alphabet coverage
test, all green.

Plank 3: model.rs — the candle trunk, every choice a Grammar identification.
Positions are ROTAT under its true name (rotary phases ARE the
Weyl-Heisenberg X; positions live on rings). Attention is a split-fuse dyad:
one fork projection (delta) to 12 heads, one fuse (mu) back, with
gate_penalty() = orthogonality pressure driving every block toward the
lossless split-fuse (add lambda*penalty to the loss). The feed-forward is
ENGAGR: two arms computed and HELD, fused without collapsing either first.
Twelve layers at d_model 144 = 12^2, heads 12, head_dim 12. At the midpoint
the stream is projected to 12-D and normalized onto S11 (the sense organ's
own hypersphere) then lifted back as a residual. Output head over the closed
100-token alphabet. Tests: logits shape and finiteness, gate penalty finite
and nonnegative, causality (a later token cannot move earlier logits). 23
tests green across the crate. CPU by default, --features cuda for the 3060.

First gradients. vita-gen labeled 150 words through the oracles (the full
dataset is 150 turns → 3768 tokens over the 100-token alphabet). vita-train,
60 steps on CPU at default config (12 layers, d=144): cross-entropy fell
5.51→4.53 monotonically and the gate penalty fell 3.52→1.95 alongside it —
the trunk learns next-token structure while every attention block moves
toward the lossless split-fuse. Weights saved (smoke checkpoint). Next: a
dataset in the thousands, the cuda feature on the 3060, and the lambda_sic
pin of the S11 core to the Scott-Grassl fiducial (the same data the spine's
vessel voice now reads on every verdict).

Full stack live. The lambda_sic pin is implemented and training: sic_penalty
drives the S11 core's points toward pairwise Born overlap 1/13 (the SIC
