vita-probe: the seed atlas, with bit-exact parity to the metal

New host-side probe: the SAME scalar forward, sampler, and gate as
mOMonadOS src/vita.rs (same libm, same baked vita_weights.bin), so every
specimen's (seed, temp) coordinates re-speak identically on the OS.
Verified: seed 666 @ 0.9 reproduces the on-metal turn byte for byte,
0.2s/turn on host vs ~30s emulated.

The probe enumerates seeds with dedup (the dedup IS the skip past the
numerically optimized core), walks a temperature schedule outward in
shells, gates every word through imasm_core, and spiders single-glyph
edits around each novel specimen through the gate alone (no forward pass).

First atlas (2000 seeds x 0.8/1.2/1.6, spidered, vita_atlas.jsonl):
- B is the trunk's dominant register at every temperature; heat drains T
  mass into N and F (the hotter shells are more foreign and more broken).
- The gate letters are PHASES: a T word's edit neighborhood is half T,
  a B word's is 80% B, an N word's 74% N, an F word's 94% F. Verdict
  regions are contiguous territories in word space, not salt-and-pepper.
- The language is vast at cap 24: novelty holds at ~80% per 200-seed
  block with no decay in sight.
