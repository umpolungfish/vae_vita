Fine temperature sweep (500 seeds per temp, 0.5 to 1.6): T falls
monotonically, N and F rise monotonically, but B is non-monotonic with a
clear peak at 0.90-0.95 (~46% of seed-space mass). At the peak F locally
dips and novelty per seed block hits its minimum: the resonant heat is
where the trunk's language is most crystallized, and where it holds
paradox rather than breaking. Figure vita_temperature_curve.png; README
carries the finding.
